INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

Use bucket utilization and crop statistics together.
A bucket scheme with many tiny buckets may preserve aspect ratio well but produce poor batching efficiency.
A very small number of buckets may improve batching efficiency but introduce excessive cropping.
For Pose-ControlNet, inspect high-crop cases visually because cropping a hand, foot, head, or entire person can damage the conditioning relationship.

The next step after this report should be to choose a final bucket scheme and then test the exact RGB/control geometric transform on representative examples.
