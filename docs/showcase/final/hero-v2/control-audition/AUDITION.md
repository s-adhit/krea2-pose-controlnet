# Hero-v2 control audition

This is a **control-only, human-review shortlist** for the seven new hero-v2
concepts. No final image was generated, and this document does not promote any
candidate to a final control. All controls below were rendered from the
authoritative `pose_targets_v3` geometry into their recorded native bucket.
`audition_candidates.json` retains the source-control path for every row.

The three candidates within a concept are ranked for review, not approved.
The same strong single-person geometry is deliberately allowed to appear in
two different concept shortlists; human selection must make the final controls
distinct if that is important to the final montage.

## Review rule and next action

Each single candidate has one authoritative person and was screened for a
readable head/torso, viable limbs, non-empty control, and native geometry.
Each duo has exactly two authoritative people and passed the initial box-scale,
separation, and low-overlap screen. The contact sheets remain the final visual
admission check: reject any candidate that is not readable at montage size.

**Next step: a human selects one final control per concept.** In particular,
the two duo concepts remain weak/cautious pools and should retain their
single-person fallbacks until their final selection passes visual review.
